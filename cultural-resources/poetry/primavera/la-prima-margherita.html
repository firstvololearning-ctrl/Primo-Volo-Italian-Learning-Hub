✍️ Lina Schwarz

Italiano: Lina Schwarz (1876–1947) fu una scrittrice e poetessa per l’infanzia. Scrisse rime e filastrocche per bambini e si interessò anche all’educazione e a iniziative sociali rivolte ai più giovani.

English: Lina Schwarz (1876–1947) was an Italian writer and poet for children. She wrote rhymes and poems for young readers and was also involved in education and social initiatives supporting children.
